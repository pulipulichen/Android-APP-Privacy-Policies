# Privacy Policy — Image Quick Cutout

**Effective date: September 28, 2026**

Image Quick Cutout is an Android app developed by Pulipuli Chen. This policy describes the app behavior represented by the public source code at the date above.

## Images and processing

The app opens an image selected with Android's Photo Picker or shared to the app by another application. It processes the image locally on the device to remove a selected solid-color background, trim transparent edges, resize and export a PNG. The app does not upload images to a developer server.

When you choose Save, the resulting PNG is written to your device's Pictures collection. When you choose Share, Android gives the selected destination app temporary access to the resulting PNG; the destination app's privacy practices then apply. Temporary files used for sharing may remain in the app's local cache until Android or the app removes them.

## Data collection and sharing

The current app does not create accounts, use advertising or analytics SDKs, request a network permission, or transmit personal data to the developer. It does not collect or sell user data. Images you select, process, save and share remain under your control on your device and in any apps you choose to share with.

On Android 8 and 9, the app requests legacy storage write permission when saving a PNG. On newer Android versions it saves through MediaStore without that permission. Android's system picker or another app may grant access to an image you select or share.

## Children and changes

The app is a general-purpose image utility and is not specifically designed for children. If its behavior or data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure should be updated before release.
